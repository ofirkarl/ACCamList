## Left out

Photo lenses (Sony, Canon, Sigma Art, ZEISS Batis/Loxia/Milvus/ZE, Panasonic, Samyang): they take filters by thread, and makers give no front for a matte box ring.
Vintage and rehoused sets (Canon FD/V35, Iron Glass, Cinescope, Cooke Panchro (Speed Panchro), Elite Optar, ZEISS Super 16), probe lenses, broadcast B4 zooms, Tribe7 Blackwing7 (no figure on its sheet), Atlas Orion other than 18/200, Cooke SP3 18/100, CP.2 15/135 and Super Speeds, Ultra Prime 10, Sigma Cine 65, Tokina 11-20, DZOFilm Pictor / Linglung / Gnosis, Dulens, NiSi Aureus, Thypoch Simera, Sirui, Blazar, Laowa Zero-D / VV.
